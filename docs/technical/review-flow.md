# Review Flow

ShipOne uses weekly review to help you decide what to do with each project.

## What the review does

The review groups the workspace into simple buckets:

- active projects
- paused projects
- finished projects
- stale projects
- projects without `nextAction`

## Why it matters

It helps answer:

- what deserves attention now?
- what can wait?
- what should be closed?

## What ShipOne suggests

The review is meant to surface simple actions:

- add a `nextAction`
- reopen a paused project
- archive or finish a project
- export a short summary

## Summary export

ShipOne can export a Markdown summary from the review.
The summary is meant to be short and readable.

## Good review rhythm

A good review should be:

- quick
- repeatable
- focused on the next step
- useful enough to act on immediately
